I work at Harvest Table in Cambridge. My manager makes me work through my 30-minute lunch break every day but only pays me for 7.5 hours instead of 8.

This looks like a wage violation. You may be owed unpaid wages.

Massachusetts law requires employers to pay workers for all time worked. If you are not fully relieved of your duties during a meal break, that time must be paid. Requiring you to work through a 30-minute break while recording only 7.5 hours is likely wage theft.

Your pay records also may not reflect your actual hours worked, which is a separate violation.
